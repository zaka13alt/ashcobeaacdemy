Admissions

Admissions are handled centrally by the Registry. Applications are considered on qualification and interview, in that order. We do not charge an application fee.

Application procedure

  1. Select a programme from the programme specifications and confirm you meet the entry requirements.
  2. Complete the online application form and upload identity documentation.
  3. Provide a transcript or evidence of prior learning where the route requires it.
  4. Attend a twenty-minute admissions interview with the programme lead.
  5. Receive a decision within ten working days. Offers state a response deadline.
  6. Complete enrolment with the Registry and pay the first fee instalment.

Funding

Instalment plans are available to all applicants at no surcharge. Employer sponsorship is accepted with a signed sponsor letter. A hardship bursary of up to £600 per year is awarded by the Registry to a limited number of enrolled students.

Recognition of prior learning

Applicants holding relevant qualifications or substantial employment experience may request credit transfer. Requests are assessed by the Academic Board and are capped at 50% of the credit volume of the programme.
