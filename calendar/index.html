Registry deadlines

  • Extenuating circumstances claims: within five working days of the affected assessment.
  • Reassessment applications: within ten working days of results publication.
  • Appeals: within twenty working days of the decision being notified.
  • Transcript requests: five working days' notice.

See the academic regulations for the full procedure behind each deadline.
