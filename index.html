Applied science education for people who intend to work in it.

Ashcobe Academy is an independent higher education provider. We teach four schools across diploma, certificate and associate levels, with taught hours, lab provision and assessment set by an academic board rather than a marketing department.

Admissions in four steps

Step 01

Choose a programme

Read the entry requirements on the programme page before applying. Certificate routes have no academic prerequisite.

Step 02

Submit the application

Online form, identity document, prior qualification transcript where applicable, and a 300-word statement.

Step 03

Admissions interview

Twenty minutes with the programme lead. Held remotely or on campus, whichever you prefer.

Step 04

Enrol and register

Offer holders complete enrolment with the Academic Registry and receive a student record number.
